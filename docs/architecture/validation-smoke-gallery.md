# Smoke validation: Gallery and HiDPI

Covers: scripts/smoke/rows/gallery.sh, scripts/smoke/rows/hidpi.sh, shell/plugins/vgs.gallery/**, scripts/smoke/Probe.qml, scripts/smoke/harness.sh

The Gallery and HiDPI smoke rows read pixels from the nested compositor through the shared harness. [validation-smoke.md](validation-smoke.md) holds the sandbox contract, and [runtime-qml.md](runtime-qml.md) holds the scale and `Qt.resolvedUrl()` facts.

- The Gallery row reads the shown boxes of `FormRow`, `DeviceRow` and `LevelOsd` in the states [components-gallery.md](components-gallery.md) names. `gallery_drawn` refuses a box with no width or height; its control flattens a form row. The keyboard Tab tour reaches the focused device row with its ring in view.
- The device list's arrow and End keys move focus to the selected row. Tab reaches the selected row's overflow and skips an unselected row to its action. Disposable Gallery copies swallow movement or make every row a Tab stop; the same focus reading refuses each defect after a positive focus check.
- The Gallery row brings the slim list, a plain `Flickable` the plugin declares, into view and reads, through the probe's `viewHolding` and the harness's `view_pointer`, that a mouse drag leaves it where it was and a wheel notch scrolls it. Its control gives the list Qt's left-button drag back through `setViewButtons`, and the same drag then scrolls it ([components.md](components.md)).
- `galleryColour` reads the first shown example of the requested type in its section. A `FormRow` without a warning keeps its hidden badge out of that reading, so the theme row compares the visible badge with its token.
- The smoke's HiDPI row, `scripts/smoke/rows/hidpi.sh`, stops the running shell, holds the first monitor at double its mode and scale 2, and starts the sandbox's tree over the default set on it: a running shell does not follow a scale change ([runtime-qml.md](runtime-qml.md)). It reads the held mode and scale from the compositor, the screen the probe's `screenOf` reads from the `vgs.themes` background at ratio 2 and half the held mode, the background's `sourceSize` at the held mode, the Gallery `ImageText` sample's pool image and magenta pixels, and a 40-row notifications inbox opened eight times, each with its first card whole under the header and inside the list (`long_inbox_cut` in `scripts/smoke/rows/notifications-keys.sh`, whose control holds it at scale 1). The `ImageText` reading requires `sourceSize` to equal `deviceSize` and `deviceSize` to equal twice `imageSize`. Its controls drop only the scale under the hold, which the hold reads as reset, and a copy of `Background.qml` that decodes at logical pixels, which requests half the held mode. It stops its shell and gives the monitor its own mode at scale 1 again.
- The Gallery row brings the `ImageText` custom-emoji sample into its viewport, reads `imageMode`, `failed`, `imageSize`, `deviceSize` and the held pool image's URL, status and `sourceSize`, then captures its box through `shot_grim` on the nested socket. The magenta threshold is one fifth of `deviceSize` squared, in device pixels, below the sample image's fill and above an alt-only text rendering. Its control draws a copy with `{ image: "", alt: ":sample:" }` over a surface fill and requires the same pixel reader to answer false.
- The Gallery row brings each discovered `VoiceOrb` example into its viewport and captures its box through `shot_grim` on the nested socket. It requires pixels in that example's tone. The VoiceOrb control first draws a blue ring in a blank slot beside the examples; hiding its shader then removes those pixels. This positive check prevents a hidden-control check from passing when nothing draws. The installed Gallery exercises the same pack from a read-only prefix. The status-cache limitation is in [runtime-qml-shaders.md](runtime-qml-shaders.md). These checks establish actual drawing, not GPU cost or no-frame-swap behavior.
