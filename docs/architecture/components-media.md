# Components: images and cards

Covers: shell/Ui/foundation/ImageText.qml, shell/Ui/foundation/ImageTextLogic.js, shell/Ui/foundation/ImagePool.qml, shell/Ui/layout/AngledCard.qml, shell/Ui/layout/CardCarousel.qml, shell/Ui/feedback/AvatarGroup.qml, shell/Ui/feedback/AvatarFace.qml, scripts/test-image-text-logic.js, scripts/qml-tests/tst_imagetext.qml, scripts/qml-tests/tst_angledcard.qml, scripts/qml-tests/tst_carousel.qml, scripts/qml-tests/tst_avatargroup.qml

What the components of `qs.Ui` that draw images and cards guarantee. The rules every component shares are in [components.md](components.md).

- `ImageText` draws StyledText with local images inline, such as a chat's custom emoji: its `segments` are `{ markup }` runs the caller has made safe and `{ image, alt }` file URLs. An image is a square of the font's line height rounded down, aligned to the line's middle, so a line holding one keeps the pitch of a line without, and it draws at full strength whatever the text's colour. A text with no image draws with Qt's own right elision. A text with images elides itself: the longest run of whole words and whole images that fits `maximumLineCount` lines, measured on a hidden copy, then one ellipsis; a word wider than a line breaks inside itself, so the text stays inside its width; no image draws past the cut or on a line it does not sit on, which Qt's elision does ([runtime-qml-text.md](runtime-qml-text.md)). Before the text names an image, `ImagePool`, a singleton, holds one asynchronous `Image` per URL and device-pixel size for every holder, so the text waits on that load and nothing decodes on the GUI thread; the entry goes with its last holder. An image whose load fails draws its alt text. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_imagetext.qml` holds each, and `scripts/test-image-text-logic.js` the tokens and the cut.
- `AngledCard` clips its content to a parallelogram whose top edge sits `skew` pixels, `angledCard.skew` by default, right of its bottom edge, or left for a negative `skew`. The clip is a `QtQuick.Shapes` parallelogram drawn as a hidden layer and used as a `MultiEffect` mask; the mask reads coverage alone, so its path keeps `ShapePath`'s default opaque fill and no theme colour reaches it. A dimmed card, by default one not `selected`, draws `angledCard.dim` inside the mask. The outline on the same edge is `borderWidth` of `border`, or `selectedBorderWidth` of `selectedBorder` while selected. `corners` states the parallelogram, so a row of cards can overlap edge to edge; the caller sizes and places the card. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_angledcard.qml` holds the corners, the mask and both paths as one opaque parallelogram leaning either way, the wash apart from `selected`, and the outline per state, under the defaults and a light theme.
- `AngledCard` is Omarchy's image picker card (`shell/plugins/image-picker/ImagePicker.qml`): its mask technique, 28 pixel lean, background wash at 0.42 and 3 pixel accent outline, each a token here. The unselected outline is `color.borderStrong`, the opaque neutral at 0.27, where Omarchy draws the foreground at 0.28 alpha: with the default palette the two are within 2 of 255 on each channel over the background, and the role moves with every strong outline. The mask keeps `MultiEffect`'s default thresholds, where Omarchy sets both to 0.3: the outline covers the mask's antialiased edge, so the card holds no edge value for a theme to set.
- `AvatarGroup` draws people as round faces in a square box `size` wide, from a list of `{ image, initials, tint }` the caller resolves, and `more`, the people past them. One person fills the box with no ring. Two to four overlap in the box's corners, clockwise from the top left: two on the diagonal, three as a triangle with its point up, four as a 2 by 2 cluster. Past four, three faces and a "+N" chip take the four places. Each face is `face` of the box across, ringed in `ring`, the surface under the group, and lies over the one before it, the chip last. A face shows its image cut to a circle once it loads, else its initials on its tint. The notifications' media slot sets every value from its own table; the gallery shows one to seven people. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_avatargroup.qml` holds the places, the ring, the stacking, the chip and the image's layer. The unit runner draws in software, where no shader effect draws, so the drawn cut is read in the sandbox.
- `CardCarousel` draws a model as a rail of `AngledCard`s: the current card expanded in the middle and slices on each side that overlap by `carousel.overlap`. Every length is a `carousel` token times one unit: the carousel's width over the reference rail, `expandedWidth + referenceSteps * (sliceWidth - overlap) + 2 * referenceMargin`, or its height over `expandedHeight`, the smaller, held between `minScale` and `maxScale`. The lean is `angledCard.skew` times the unit. A side shows the slices that fit whole beside the expanded card, so nothing draws outside the carousel. The cards within `band` of the shown ones are built, and the rest hold no content. `delegate` is one card's content; its root declares `modelData` and `decodeSize`, and may declare `current` to receive whether that card is selected. A built slot can survive a model change at the same index, so `modelData` is rebound after creation. `decodeSize` is the drawn size in device pixels for the current card and its two neighbours, with the longer side at most `decodeCap`, and the slice size for every other card. The neighbours therefore decode ahead of a step. A click lands in the parallelogram that holds it, the nearer card's where two meet: on a slice it selects it, and on the current card it emits `activated`. Left, Right, Tab, Shift+Tab, Home, End and the wheel in 120-unit notches step through `step`, which wraps at the ends. A caller that sets `tabSteps` false keeps Tab and Shift+Tab for its own surface-level navigation. The rail shows one event-loop turn after the carousel is visible with a size, so the first frame is the settled layout. After that a new index within the shown slices of the index the rail is drawn at glides there over `carousel.duration`. A farther move, such as a wrap or Home and End across the model, lands at once, and `motion.scale` 0 stills every move. A card is shown while within the shown slices of the drawn index or of the new one, and built while within the band of either, so a glide keeps the cards it leaves and builds the ones it reaches. The rail clips to the carousel. A delegate that does not build with both properties leaves its card empty, and the carousel logs one `CardCarousel: no content index=<n>` warning for it. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_carousel.qml` holds each of these at the reference size, at a size the height limits and at one the width limits.
- `CardCarousel` keeps no thumbnail cache: the band bounds the decoded images. A thumbnail cache is added only if `scripts/sample-shell-memory.sh`, run while paging the wallpaper browser's All view, shows memory growth the band does not bound.
- `CardCarousel` takes Omarchy's image picker numbers (`shell/plugins/image-picker/ImagePicker.qml`) as the `carousel` defaults: the 768 by 475 card, 108 by 432 slices 30 over each other, and the 13 slice steps and 20 pixel margins of its reference rail. Omarchy draws the rail at that one size, draws 16 slices a side past the rail's edge, and keeps every image it has loaded. VGS scales the rail by v1's unit and clamp, 0.35 to 2 over a 1822 pixel reference, and keeps v1's band of two cards past the shown ones. It decodes slices at their size, as v1 did, and hands the current card and its neighbours the drawn card size times the screen's device pixel ratio, capped only at 4096 on the longer side. The cap scales the longer side, so the decode keeps the card's shape, where v1 capped each side alone. Omarchy has no animation and moves the selection at once; VGS moves the rail over `motion.duration.normal`. Both hold the rail hidden until its layout settles.
