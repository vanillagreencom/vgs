# Components: menus and overlays

Covers: shell/Ui/overlay/**, shell/Ui/controls/Select.qml, scripts/smoke/rows/overlays.sh, scripts/smoke/fixtures/plugins/acme.overlays/**, scripts/qml-tests/tst_overlays.qml

What the components of `qs.Ui` that open over the surface under them guarantee. The rules every component shares are in [components.md](components.md), and the embedded scroll bar their lists scroll under is in [components-layout.md](components-layout.md).

- `Menu` is the one menu and dropdown. Its entries and a `Select`'s fill the list inside the border, so the highlight and a chosen entry's fill meet the border at the top and both sides. A `Select`'s list opens on the field's edges and has the field's width, and an entry's text starts where the field's text starts ([design-layout.md § Lists in a popover](design-layout.md#lists-in-a-popover)). Under a rounded corner the list is cut to the window's rounded interior by a `MultiEffect` mask, `ListMask`, so a row half scrolled past the top, its highlight, a chosen row's fill and the bar's thumb stay inside the curve; a square list draws no layer. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_overlays.qml` holds this under a square and a rounded theme. The unit runner draws no shader effect ([validation-qml-unit.md](validation-qml-unit.md)), so the test reads the mask's shape and holds it inside the interior. An entry keeps the bar's strip by the bar's own overflow test, so the strip shows exactly while the bar does. Past `menu.maxHeight` its entries scroll with the highlight in view. Typed letters highlight the first reachable entry, enabled and shown, whose text starts with them, and the letters are kept for `menu.typeahead` milliseconds. Opening shows the top and highlights the first checked entry, or the first reachable entry when none is checked; a checked `MenuItem` draws a check mark in `menu.item.check` at its end. A menu is at most `menu.maxWidth` wide, and a menu, the list of a `Select` and a tooltip are never wider than their output's room, the output less `size.window.gutter` a side (`OverlayState.room`, which summoned popups and the Settings window read too); a `MenuItem`'s title elides before its shortcut, which sits in a trailing column. A `Tooltip` wraps inside `tooltip.maxWidth` and stays inside its output. `TitleButton` names the current choice in a typography role with a down caret `titleButton.gap` after it; at rest the caret alone marks the menu. While it is hovered or its menu is open, the text and the caret take `titleButton.hover` and an underline shows `titleButton.underlineGap` under the text; the underline also shows while the button holds keyboard focus. A click, Space, Enter or Down toggles the `Menu` declared inside it. Given less width than it needs, the text elides and the caret stays inside.
- An overlay (`Popover`, `Tooltip`, `Menu`, the list of `Select`) is a Quickshell `PopupWindow` anchored to the item that declares it, under its bottom-left edge with the theme's gap: its own surface, so it leaves a bar of any height. `Popover` composes `Pane`, fits its content until `popover.maxHeightShare` of the available screen height, then scrolls the body. `Popover`, `Menu` and the list take a focus grab, so they hold the keys and close on a press outside and on Escape, which `scripts/smoke/rows/overlays.sh` reads for each beside a copy of each without the grab that the same press leaves open; `Tooltip` takes none, opens after `tooltip.delay` while the pointer rests, stays closed while `OverlayState` counts an open overlay, and after a press on its item stays closed until the pointer leaves the item, so it never covers what the press opened. `AnchorTracker` watches the anchor and its ancestors: a move updates the anchor and a hide closes the popup. `Menu` and `Select` hold their own keys, since the surface a control sits in may take no keyboard focus. [D018](../decisions/D018-overlays-are-quickshell-popups.md) records the choice.
