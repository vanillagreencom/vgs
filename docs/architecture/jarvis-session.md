# Jarvis session

Covers: shell/plugins/vgs.jarvis/Session.js, shell/plugins/vgs.jarvis/backend/session-runner.js, scripts/test-jarvis-session.js, scripts/test-jarvis-session-runner.js

## Session

`Session.js::reduce` owns all transitions. `Session.js::REGIONS` owns the tagged state shape; `phaseOf` owns phase priority. The reducer reads time only from the event. It returns a new state and ordered effects without changing its inputs.

- `shell/plugins/vgs.jarvis/backend/session-runner.js::SessionRunner` owns callback stamping, the event queue and the next deadline. Synchronous adapter callbacks enter the queue after the current transition's effects. Mute storage completes before its changed state publishes. Failed storage suppresses that publication, delivers the remaining effects, then throws the original error. Capture close and playback flush cannot depend on storage. It replaces its deadline timer after each drain. Lease loss forces brain close, releases the timer and calls the speech port's `release`, so no speech session, open or finalizing, holds the daemon. Late callbacks cannot rearm a closed owner.
- The daemon supplies real [audio ports](jarvis-audio.md) and the [chained engine's](jarvis-engine.md) speech and brain ports. The engine's gate stays unconfigured without a speech row. An acquisition through an unavailable port throws an invariant error, never a simulated success. It supplies real [router ports](jarvis-approval.md) for tools and approval and registers the [window and application executors](jarvis-desktop-tools.md), [guarded input and installed computer help](jarvis-input.md) and [clipboard, media and notify executors](jarvis-tools.md). Every hello produces a real snapshot event; every resulting state reaches the service through the wire and becomes the plugin's `detail` status.
- The runner stamps capture, transcript, brain, playback, tool and approval callbacks with their effect's identity. A callback must match the region's live operation and generation. Session counts discarded callbacks. Cancellation acknowledgments and eventual tool outcomes retain their original identity across stop; they cannot update a newer turn.
- Conversation start, end and session-setting changes invalidate generation identity. `SESSION_SETTINGS` owns which settings end the session, including mode and brain. Next-capture settings do not end it. Only settings with both wire consumers enter hello.
- A repeated held edge changes nothing. A new press after release and the explicit interrupt event use `Session.js::interrupt`. It retires the previous collection and held approval, cancels thinking and flushes playback. A delayed final from the previous hold cannot close or replace the new hold. Release without hold demand changes nothing. Toggle collapse uses event time. The plan's debounce and thinking, approval and cancellation deadlines are behavioral rules, not measured latency budgets.
- Interrupt preserves a running tool. Stop requests tool cancellation only while the tool offers it. No new proposal starts in an interrupted conversation, alongside a running tool or while approval is held. Policy judges whether a call needs approval. The router owns the held call and grants. Session judges confirmation identity, drawing delay, expiry and physical source, then retires the hold before tool-start.
- Capture requires an up gate, unmuted state, a shown indicator and no fault or turn cancellation. [Half-duplex admission](jarvis-audio-duplex.md#admission-and-resume) defines the playback boundary. Mute remains `muting` until capture acknowledges close.
- `indicatorWanted` derives the [bubble's demand](jarvis-bubble.md#demand-and-presentation) before capture opens. It adds no second capture judge.
- `collect-failed` is live on the collecting turn: a transcription that fails after its capture closed sets an error fault and ends the conversation. `brain-ended` is live on the thinking turn: it ends the conversation with the brain's keyed reason and no fault, so the next conversation can start. The chained engine sends it at the history bound.
- Thinking expiry retires its held approval before cancellation, even if the approval deadline is later. Thinking cancellation retains its operation until acknowledgment or its deadline. An acknowledged cancel closes the adapter only when the conversation ended; an interrupted conversation keeps it, so its history survives barge-in. The deadline always emits adapter close. Arriving callbacks also check expiry, so a delayed timer cannot admit late content. Each tool proposal carries its registered executor's deadline. An expired tool reports `unknown` but keeps the serial slot until its eventual completion, failure or unknown outcome. The outcome effect names the original brain operation even after stop. The router audits outcomes and supplies the brain result port, which the chained engine consumes.

The `brain` region retains the acquired adapter identity after a response completes. A later send names that same owner while its turn receives a new operation. Conversation end closes a completed adapter immediately; an active response keeps its cancellation deadline. Lease loss forces either owner closed. Tool outcomes still name the original turn and tool operation after adapter close, not a newer conversation.

The snapshot names the daemon's engine in the `engine` region, `chained` or `duplex`; a change ends the conversation. A duplex conversation's first capture opens the `speech` session before capture, collects no utterance, and closes it when the conversation ends: gracefully, or by abort on lease loss. `speak` queues one reply in `speech.reply` until playback is idle, the conversation is active and no talk key is held. The reply then follows the same [half-duplex admission](jarvis-audio-duplex.md#admission-and-resume) as a chained reply: capture closes before playback starts. Interrupt emits `speech-flush` and drops a queued reply. `speech-idle` ends the conversation; `speech-failed` records the engine's keyed fault and ends it. `transcript` callbacks become `transcript` effects for the wire. Session admits one from the open speech session, or an `assistant` caption from the live thinking turn, the chained engine's reply words. Anything else is a discarded callback. [The GPT-Live engine](jarvis-live.md) is the duplex port.

The `input` region retains demand while capture opens, closes or waits for playback. `conversation` separates active, interrupted and ended lifetimes. `indicator` records the mapped capture prerequisite. `duplex` reports the implemented audio mode. Playback admission, tool cancellation and tool deadline are tagged values inside their owning regions. These regions implement no audio process.

## Evidence

- `scripts/test-jarvis-session.js` tests transitions, ordered event pairs and state-shape refusals. The matrix retains seed-owner callbacks and discovers effects produced by the first event for live second callbacks. It covers capture, collection, brain, playback, tool and approval callbacks, including every deadline owner. Each created pair pins a transition or effect. Independent controls omit each callback family or deadline pair, or change created identities into stale seed identities. Separate mutations break hold replacement, approval retirement, retained brain ownership, identity, muted capture and the other lifetime rules. Duplex rows pin session order, held-reply admission, flush, resumption, close modes, idle, failure, stale speech callbacks, captions and engine change, each with its mutant; a chained row admits the thinking turn's assistant caption and drops a user caption, an ended turn's and a cancelling turn's, with a mutant each for the admission, its role and its phase; the matrix includes duplex seeds and the speech-open callbacks.
- `scripts/test-jarvis-session-runner.js` uses in-memory ports and an injectable clock. Its stand-in brain connection stays open after done until the close port releases it. It proves lease, stop and setting teardown after completed responses, the speech release on lease loss, callback identity, synchronous callback ordering, deadline replacement, eventual tool outcomes and timer release. `failed-mute-capture` and `failed-mute-playback` prove active resources release before a storage error propagates and stay released after close. Independent controls drop cleanup, suppress the error or publish the failed transition. These pure suites start no child or socket.
