# Components

Covers: shell/Ui/foundation/**, shell/Ui/controls/**, shell/Ui/feedback/**, shell/Ui/layout/**, shell/Ui/overlay/**, shell/Ui/icons/**, shell/Ui/qmldir, scripts/smoke/rows/overlays.sh, scripts/smoke/fixtures/plugins/acme.overlays/**, shell/Ui/AGENTS.md, scripts/vendor-lucide, scripts/test-lucide-data.js, scripts/test-icon-bounds.js, scripts/qml-tests/**, scripts/check-pointer-cursor.py, scripts/test-check-pointer-cursor.py

What each component of `qs.Ui` guarantees. The tokens they draw from and the layers they sit in are in [design-system.md](design-system.md). The base controls are in [components-controls.md](components-controls.md), layout and feedback components are in [components-layout.md](components-layout.md), images, cards and the voice ring are in [components-media.md](components-media.md), the overlays and the menus' scrolling are in [components-overlays.md](components-overlays.md), and the list motion of `ListCursor` and `ListEntrance` is in [motion.md](motion.md).

- A control that takes a name (`variant`, `size`, `role`, `tone`, `level`) logs an error naming the component and the value for an unknown name and draws its default. A control draws `FocusRing` while `visualFocus` holds, so a keyboard user sees the ring and a pointer user does not; the ring takes its corner from the item it fills. A control's side padding under a rounded corner is `Theme.controlPadding`, and every corner clearance steps by `inset.cornerStep`. A control sets `Accessible.name` from its text; `IconButton` requires `label` and logs an error without one. A disabled control draws at `opacity.disabled`. Every animation reads a `motion` token, so `motion.scale` of 0 stills the shell.
- Keyboard support follows [keyboard.md](keyboard.md). Button-like controls activate on Enter, Return and Space. Composite controls keep one tab stop and move inside with arrows. `focusPreview` previews the keyboard focus state without moving focus.
- Every element that takes a click shows the pointing hand while the pointer rests on it and it is enabled: it declares `PointerCursor`, a passive `HoverHandler` and the one place the shell names `Qt.PointingHandCursor`. Each control of `qs.Ui` that takes a click declares it, and so does a plugin's own `MouseArea`. A disabled control shows the arrow, since Qt picks no cursor from a disabled item, and a text field keeps its I-beam. A scroll bar keeps the arrow, as Qt's own does, and so does an area that takes a click away from a surface, such as `Scrim`. `scripts/check-pointer-cursor.py` refuses a `MouseArea` that takes a button, a `TapHandler` or a Qt Quick Templates button or slider in shipped QML without a `PointerCursor`. An exemption is a `// pointer-cursor-exempt: <reason>` line directly above the element. `scripts/test-check-pointer-cursor.py` plants one violation per rule. `vgs-plugin check` runs the rule on a third-party plugin's tree; `vgsh plugin validate` and `plugin add` run the manifest judge alone, since the shell loads a plugin whatever its cursors. The Qt and Hyprland facts it rests on are in [runtime-pointer.md](runtime-pointer.md). `scripts/smoke/rows/manager.sh`, `gallery.sh`, `overlays.sh` and `launcher.sh` read the shape the compositor shows over each kind of control.
- Omarchy (`basecamp/omarchy`, branch `quattro`, read 2026-09-29) sets the same shapes: the hand on its buttons, rows and bar widgets, the arrow where a control is disabled or busy, and the arrow on its click-away area and stock scroll bars. It writes `cursorShape: Qt.PointingHandCursor` into 44 `MouseArea`s one by one and has no check. VGS names the hand once and checks every click area: the literals it had reached seven plugin files and no `qs.Ui` control.
- A surface scrolls by the wheel, the keys, its scroll bar and a touch drag, never by a mouse drag: a mouse press and drag selects text where text is selectable and otherwise does nothing, so a drag across a window, such as an area screenshot's selection, leaves it where it was. `ScrollArea` sets `acceptedButtons: Qt.NoButton` once for every use, and each `Flickable`, `ListView` or `GridView` a control or a plugin declares itself sets it too; the Qt fact is in [runtime-pointer.md](runtime-pointer.md). `scripts/check-pointer-cursor.py` refuses one without it as `mouse-drag`, with no exemption, in shipped QML and, through `vgs-plugin check`, in a third-party plugin. `scripts/smoke/rows/settings.sh` and `gallery.sh` read a real mouse drag leave a view still, a wheel step move it and, in Settings, the Tab focus scroll its row into view, and restore the drag on the same view as their control. Omarchy (`basecamp/omarchy`, branch `quattro` at `821ae58`, read 2026-10-02) sets `acceptedButtons` on none of the 18 `Flickable`, `ListView`, `GridView` and `ScrollView` elements in its QML and binds `interactive` in 9 of them, so its lists drag with the mouse; VGS turns the drag off, as the Qt reference advises for a mouse and touchpad interface.
- `ListItem` owns the row highlight box, the icon size, the icon-to-text gap, the trailing-control gap and the two-line rhythm. The box sits on its container's inset edge, and the icon and text sit inside the row padding. A row that names its list's `ListCursor` in `cursor` draws no fill of its own: the cursor draws the highlight, the row hands it itself while `highlighted`, emits `pointed` for a hover the cursor lets through, and enters through `ListEntrance` as it is created unless `enters` is false. `MenuItem` and the list of `Select` draw their highlight the same way, so a menu, a dropdown and a pure list move one highlight under the keyboard and the pointer ([motion.md](motion.md)). `textStart` is where its text starts, for content a caller lines up under it.
- `Field` is an unboxed label, hint and control row, and its control row is a `FormRow`, the one key/value row. Its default horizontal padding is zero, so its label sits on its container's content edge and its control ends on that edge; a theme may indent all fields through `field.paddingX`.
- `Label` draws one typography role and sets both `font.weight` and `font.variableAxes`, since a variable font moves on the axis alone and a static family on the weight alone. Letter spacing is stated in em and set in pixels. A role's line height is a font-size multiple, floored at the font line box rounded up to a whole pixel. `Label` publishes the line box, half-leading, capital centre and optical width.
`shell/Ui` controls are covered by [components-controls.md](components-controls.md). Feedback components and layout containers are covered by [components-layout.md](components-layout.md). A new base component is added to its topic file unless it belongs to media, overlays or motion.
