# Components

Covers: shell/Ui/foundation/**, shell/Ui/controls/**, shell/Ui/feedback/**, shell/Ui/layout/**, shell/Ui/overlay/**, shell/Ui/icons/**, shell/Ui/qmldir, scripts/smoke/rows/overlays.sh, scripts/smoke/fixtures/plugins/acme.overlays/**, shell/Ui/AGENTS.md, scripts/vendor-lucide, scripts/test-lucide-data.js, scripts/qml-tests/**

What each component of `qs.Ui` guarantees. The tokens they draw from and the layers they sit in are in [design-system.md](design-system.md).

- A control that takes a name (`variant`, `size`, `role`, `tone`, `level`) logs an error naming the component and the value for an unknown name and draws its default. A control draws `FocusRing` while `visualFocus` holds, so a keyboard user sees the ring and a pointer user does not. A control sets `Accessible.name` from its text; `IconButton` requires `label` and logs an error without one. A disabled control draws at `opacity.disabled`. Every animation reads a `motion` token, so `motion.scale` of 0 stills the shell.
- `Label` draws one typography role and sets both `font.weight` and `font.variableAxes`, since a variable font moves on the axis alone and a static family on the weight alone. Letter spacing is stated in em and set in pixels.
- The bar's built-ins draw their text in `text.bar`, a mono chrome role with line height 1, so a label's line box is the font's own height and centring the box centres the glyphs. `bar.item` holds each item's `paddingX`, the `gap` between the items of one widget, the `iconGap` between an item's icon and its text and the `radius`: a workspace pill is its label plus `paddingX` a side, never narrower than `size.control.sm`, and its label's line height plus `space.xs` tall. Every built-in sits on the bar's vertical centre; `scripts/smoke/rows/bar.sh` reads the pills, their labels and the clock back and holds each to it within one pixel, and `scripts/smoke/rows/manager.sh` does the same for the Settings gear.
- `Icon` draws Lucide path data from `shell/Ui/icons/Lucide.js`, which `scripts/vendor-lucide` writes as path commands. The shape is drawn in the data's box and scaled as an item, so `icon.stroke` is the same number of pixels at every size.
- Radios under one parent are exclusive, as the template makes them. `TextField` draws its action buttons as children of the field, not of its background, because the control puts the background under itself and the input takes every press on it.
- `ScrollArea`, `Menu` and the list of `Select` scroll under one embedded bar, the module's internal `ScrollBar`, drawn from the `scrollArea` tokens. It sits in a gutter a scroll area's content always leaves free, so the layout never moves when an overflow starts, shows while hovered or scrolling and fades after, drags by a thumb with a minimum length and pages on a press on its track; `scripts/qml-tests/tst_scroll.qml` holds each.
- `Menu` is the one menu and dropdown: past `menu.maxHeight` its entries scroll with the highlight in view, typed letters jump to the first entry that starts with them, and opening highlights the checked entry, which draws a check mark. `TitleButton` names the current choice over an underline and a caret and opens the `Menu` declared inside it.
- An overlay (`Popover`, `Tooltip`, `Menu`, the list of `Select`) is a Quickshell `PopupWindow` anchored to the item that declares it, under its bottom-left edge with the theme's gap: its own surface, so it leaves a bar of any height. `Popover`, `Menu` and the list take a focus grab, so they hold the keys and close on a press outside and on Escape, which `scripts/smoke/rows/overlays.sh` reads for each beside a copy of each without the grab that the same press leaves open; `Tooltip` takes none, opens after `tooltip.delay` while the pointer rests, and stays closed while `OverlayState` counts an open overlay. `AnchorTracker` watches the anchor and its ancestors: a move updates the anchor and a hide closes the popup. `Menu` and `Select` hold their own keys, since the surface a control sits in may take no keyboard focus. [D018](../decisions/D018-overlays-are-quickshell-popups.md) records the choice.
- `Dialog` is the one confirmation card and no overlay: its host places it. Enter presses the focused action, as `gum confirm` does. Its header states the rest; `scripts/qml-tests/tst_dialog.qml` holds each.
- `AngledCard` clips its content to a parallelogram whose top edge sits `skew` pixels, `angledCard.skew` by default, right of its bottom edge, or left for a negative `skew`. The clip is a `QtQuick.Shapes` parallelogram drawn as a hidden layer and used as a `MultiEffect` mask; the mask reads coverage alone, so its path keeps `ShapePath`'s default opaque fill and no theme colour reaches it. A dimmed card, by default one not `selected`, draws `angledCard.dim` inside the mask. The outline on the same edge is `borderWidth` of `border`, or `selectedBorderWidth` of `selectedBorder` while selected. `corners` states the parallelogram, so a row of cards can overlap edge to edge; the caller sizes and places the card. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_angledcard.qml` holds the corners, the mask and both paths as one opaque parallelogram leaning either way, the wash apart from `selected`, and the outline per state, under the defaults and a light theme.
- `AngledCard` is Omarchy's image picker card (`shell/plugins/image-picker/ImagePicker.qml`): its mask technique, 28 pixel lean, background wash at 0.42 and 3 pixel accent outline, each a token here. The unselected outline is `color.borderStrong`, the opaque neutral at 0.27, where Omarchy draws the foreground at 0.28 alpha: with the default palette the two are within 2 of 255 on each channel over the background, and the role moves with every strong outline. The mask keeps `MultiEffect`'s default thresholds, where Omarchy sets both to 0.3: the outline covers the mask's antialiased edge, so the card holds no edge value for a theme to set.
- `Scrim` fills its parent with `color.scrim`, takes the presses, the hover and the wheel on it, so nothing under it answers, and emits `clicked`, so the surface declared after it can close on a click away. Omarchy's image picker washes at 0.5 of the background; `color.scrim` stays the one scrim role, at 0.6. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_scrim.qml` holds the fill, the colour under two themes, the click, and the press, hover and wheel kept from the controls under it.
- `CodeLine` shows a command or path to copy: the text in the `code` role, wrapped anywhere so a long line shows whole, and a Copy `IconButton` that puts the text on the clipboard and shows a check mark for `codeLine.confirm`. The text is read-only and takes no focus; `scripts/qml-tests/tst_codeline.qml` holds each.
- `CardCarousel` draws a model as a rail of `AngledCard`s: the current card expanded in the middle and slices on each side that overlap by `carousel.overlap`. Every length is a `carousel` token times one unit: the carousel's width over the reference rail, `expandedWidth + referenceSteps * (sliceWidth - overlap) + 2 * referenceMargin`, or its height over `expandedHeight`, the smaller, held between `minScale` and `maxScale`. The lean is `angledCard.skew` times the unit. A side shows the slices that fit whole beside the expanded card, so nothing draws outside the carousel. The cards within `band` of the shown ones are built, and the rest hold no content. `delegate` is one card's content; its root declares `modelData` and `decodeSize`. `decodeSize` is the drawn size in device pixels for the current card and its two neighbours, with the longer side at most `decodeCap`, and the slice size for every other card. The neighbours therefore decode ahead of a step. A click lands in the parallelogram that holds it, the nearer card's where two meet: on a slice it selects it, and on the current card it emits `activated`. Left, Right, Tab, Shift+Tab, Home, End and the wheel in 120-unit notches step through `step`, which wraps at the ends. The rail shows one event-loop turn after the carousel is visible with a size, so the first frame is the settled layout. After that a new index within the shown slices of the index the rail is drawn at glides there over `carousel.duration`. A farther move, such as a wrap or Home and End across the model, lands at once, and `motion.scale` 0 stills every move. A card is shown while within the shown slices of the drawn index or of the new one, and built while within the band of either, so a glide keeps the cards it leaves and builds the ones it reaches. The rail clips to the carousel. A delegate that does not build with both properties leaves its card empty, and the carousel logs one `CardCarousel: no content index=<n>` warning for it. `scripts/qml-tests/tst_carousel.qml` holds each of these at the reference size, at a size the height limits and at one the width limits.
- `CardCarousel` keeps no thumbnail cache: the band bounds the decoded images. A thumbnail cache is added only if `scripts/sample-shell-memory.sh`, run while paging the wallpaper browser's All view, shows memory growth the band does not bound.
- `CardCarousel` takes Omarchy's image picker numbers (`shell/plugins/image-picker/ImagePicker.qml`) as the `carousel` defaults: the 768 by 475 card, 108 by 432 slices 30 over each other, and the 13 slice steps and 20 pixel margins of its reference rail. Omarchy draws the rail at that one size, draws 16 slices a side past the rail's edge, and keeps every image it has loaded. VGS scales the rail by v1's unit and clamp, 0.35 to 2 over a 1822 pixel reference, and keeps v1's band of two cards past the shown ones. It decodes slices at their size, as v1 did, and hands the current card and its neighbours v1's full decode capped at 2560; the cap scales the longer side, so the decode keeps the card's shape, where v1 capped each side alone. Omarchy has no animation and moves the selection at once; VGS moves the rail over `motion.duration.normal`. Both hold the rail hidden until its layout settles.
